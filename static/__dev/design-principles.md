
SkillConnect is a neighborhood trust network: the warmth of a local noticeboard refined with the clarity of a modern service directory. Use light mode as the default, with optional dark-mode tokens ready. Keep the rhythm generous on discovery and compact in dashboards. Warm ivory and sand surfaces, deep forest-green as the single signature accent, ink-like text, subtle borders, soft medium radii, and restrained shadows should feel welcoming and dependable rather than corporate or construction-themed. Use a characterful editorial display face for headlines and a highly legible sans-serif for controls and body copy. Use small status colors only when they carry meaning; prioritize clear hierarchy, human details, and visible trust signals.
